# Toolsmith — working template

Use the [shared Trading rules](trading_design_directions.md). Toolsmith sells Pickaxes, Axes, Shovels and Hoes. Its catalog is under review.

| Stage | Sells | Buys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Novice | Candidate: two distinct unenchanted Copper/Iron tools | Fixed Flint and one of Coal or Charcoal |
| Apprentice | One unenchanted Copper/Iron tool. A Novice family may repeat in a different material. | One draw from the remaining fuel, Copper Ingots and Iron Ingots |
| Journeyman | One tool drawn from any of the four families, in Copper, Iron or Gold, with a few low-potency enchantments | Candidate: Iron/Gold Ingots or a plain Copper/Iron tool. Exclude Iron Ingots if selected at Apprentice. |
| Expert | One Iron, Gold or Diamond tool with some medium-potency enchantments, or the [shared smith ingot alternative](trading_design_directions.md#working-price-and-stock-calibration) | Gold Ingots, Diamonds or one Iron/Gold tool, with optional low-potency Basic enchantments |
| Master | One Diamond or Netherite tool with some high-potency enchantments. Review former special material alternatives. | Repeat Expert's pool, excluding the selected option |

**Approved family draw:** Pickaxes, Axes, Shovels and Hoes have equal weight among eligible families. The two Novice offers use different families. At Apprentice, exclude the family and material combinations already offered at Novice. All four families are eligible for each equipment sale at Journeyman, Expert and Master.

Flint is purchase-only. Apprentice can skip the remaining fuel. Sold Axes use Efficiency, Unbreaking, Fortune, Silk Touch and Mending only. Fortune and Silk Touch are incompatible. Choose remaining early materials, Master sale alternatives and purchase exclusion granularity during catalog review.

See the [shared stock calibration](trading_design_directions.md#working-price-and-stock-calibration). Prior catalogs, fuel and metal stock estimates, and the time-to-sell calculation are in the [22 November snapshot](../research_index.md#backups-anteriores).
